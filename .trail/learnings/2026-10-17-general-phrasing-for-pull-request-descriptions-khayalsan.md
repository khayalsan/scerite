## Decided
Write pull request descriptions in general phrasing, such as “Selection persists between launches” and “Lets you choose…”. Avoid first-person sentences.

## Tried and ruled out
First-person descriptions such as “My selection persists” do not match the user's preferred PR voice.

## Watch out
Keep descriptions concise and clear from the user's point of view without referring to an agent.
